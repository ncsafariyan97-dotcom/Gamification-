🏆 CHALLENGE TIME

Your Challenge 🎯

You have 5 minutes to design and prepare a short Kids English Activity.

Choose a simple topic:

🐶 Animals 🍎 Food 🎨 Colors 🧸 Toys 🏃 Actions
1️⃣ Learning Objective What exactly should students learn or practice?
2️⃣ Student Interaction Pair Work / Group Work / Whole-class Interaction
3️⃣ Movement How will students move, react or physically participate?
4️⃣ Short & Clear Instructions Prepare the exact instructions you would give your students.
5️⃣ Kids-Appropriate Make sure it fits the students' age and level.
6️⃣ Student Talking Time – STT Students should actively use English.
7️⃣ Fun + Purpose Fun + Interactive + Effective
🎤

Now, Teach It!

You have 2–3 minutes.

Demonstrate your Activity as if you were teaching a real Kids class.

Teacher

👧🏻👦🏻 Other Teachers

Students

Start your Activity.

2️⃣ Instructions

Give your instructions.

3️⃣ Demonstrate

Show students what to do.

4️⃣ Monitor

Monitor & Interact.

5️⃣ Switch

Let another Teacher demonstrate.
